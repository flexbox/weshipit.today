Offline-first in 45 s: four directions

Same frame in every direction: beat S5, no signal, three visits saved in phone storage, queue full. Judged on four things: can a founder read it on a phone, does it look like weshipit.today, does it show the real thing, and does motion have something to carry.

Decision: B, Warm plan

Why B wins. Its warm paper ground carries the brand's blueprint line language (grid, thin strokes, mono labels) at 14.7:1 ink contrast, and a single deep-cyan accent marks "saved". The brand guide recommends the light variant for LinkedIn feeds, where founders will meet this film. The phone UI stays legible as real UI. The diagram uses the same strokes as the UI, so a tap's dot can turn into a queue chip and then a server row without any change in style.

Why not A. It's the most on-brand, but cyan on navy invites glow, and thin cyan lines drop below 3:1 once the video is compressed and watched on a small screen. We keep it for developer audiences (see tools/podcast-motion/legend-state.html).

Why not C. The real UI reads instantly, but it hides the architecture, which is the whole point. You'd see that a save worked, never where it went.

Why not D. The swimlane is the most accurate picture of order, but founders read it as a Gantt chart. We take one idea from it: the queue slots are numbered 1, 2, 3 left to right, so order stays visible.
